Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026

AgentGate connects assistants to browser tasks under permissions you choose. This policy covers our website, approval app, browser extension, and cloud browser service.

Who handles your information

AgentGate is operated by David Challoner. For privacy questions or requests, contact dchalloner@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account and device information. Account identifiers, a name and email address you provide, browser connection details, approval credentials, settings, and connection times.
  • Google sign-in. When this option is available and you choose it, Google provides an account identifier, email address and verification status, and basic profile information such as your name and picture. We use this information to sign you in and associate your devices with the same AgentGate account. The requested scopes are openid, email, and profile.
  • Browser tasks. Your task instructions, allowed websites and actions, approvals, decision records, task status, and results. Cloud browser execution processes the pages it visits, including page content, screenshots, and information you enter.
  • Saved website logins. If you sign in through the cloud browser or share selected logins from the extension, we process the associated cookies and selected website storage so your cloud browser can stay signed in.
  • Service information. Network requests expose information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request times to our hosting provider. We also process operational errors and usage information needed to operate the service and investigate problems.

How we use information

We use information to operate your account, recognize authorized devices, perform requested browser tasks, apply your permissions, restore selected logins, provide support, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse. Google sign-in information is used for account identity and related support and security. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or training general-purpose AI models.

Where information goes

Cloudflare hosts the website, coordinator, account storage, and cloud browser. Cloudflare and the service operator are trusted with the raw information processed by the cloud browser, including its pages and saved logins. Saved cloud login profiles are encrypted in account storage.

Your connected assistant receives filtered task views and results through its AgentGate connection. The assistant provider handles that information under its own policies.

Classifiers and optional inference providers process task evidence to evaluate relevance and actions. A paired device can receive bounded cloud task evidence. If you configure remote inference, redacted candidate content is sent to the provider you select. That provider's policies also apply.

Websites you visit receive browser requests, form entries, and actions performed on your behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse or protect the service and its users.

Browser storage and cookies

AgentGate uses cookies and browser storage to maintain sign-in and authorization state, save settings, and hold device approval keys. The extension accesses website data for the browser tasks and login transfers you authorize. Website session cookies saved in the cloud browser let those websites recognize your session.

Retention and your choices

Account settings, connection records, task history, and encrypted login profiles can remain stored after a task ends. Expiration of a permission or closure of a task does not by itself delete all associated records. We retain information for service operation, support, security, and legal obligations.

You can revoke assistant and browser connections, stop tasks, and remove selected shared website logins in AgentGate. You can revoke Google access in your Google Account connections. Revocation stops future access through that connection; contact us to request deletion of information already held by AgentGate.

Contact dchalloner@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of your account information. We verify account ownership before acting on a request. Copies held by assistant providers, inference providers, and websites are subject to their own controls and retention policies.

Children and changes

AgentGate is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

We update this page when our practices change. We provide notice and seek fresh consent before using Google user data for a new purpose that requires it.
