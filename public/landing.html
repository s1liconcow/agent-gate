The assistant sees the work.
You see the boundary.

Cloudflare runs the browser and processes its pages and saved logins. The acting assistant receives filtered task views through MCP. Owner Live View gives you direct access for sign-in and MFA.

Outside the assistant interface

  • Full pages and screenshots
  • Saved logins, cookies and MFA
  • Other tasks and browser tabs
  • Arbitrary browser scripts

Inside the task

  • Signed website origins and time limits
  • Filtered text and control references
  • Recorded decisions under your setting
  • Owner confirmation when your level requires it

The cloud browser’s login profile is encrypted in account storage. Cloudflare and the service operator remain trusted with the raw pages and logins. A paired desktop extension has a separate local data path. Optional remote inference sends hard-redacted candidates to its configured provider. Read the cloud browser data flow.
